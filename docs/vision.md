# VitoCare-01

## Project Description

VitoCare is an offline-first, community-driven healthcare platform designed to support people living in rural, tribal, and underserved communities. The platform helps patients manage chronic diseases by providing medicine reminders, appointment tracking, and volunteer-assisted healthcare support.

The goal is to ensure that every patient receives continuous healthcare even in areas where hospitals, clinics, or internet connectivity are limited.

---

# Problem Statement

Millions of people living in rural and tribal areas struggle to manage chronic diseases because healthcare facilities are far away, internet access is unreliable, and regular doctor visits are difficult.

Many patients forget to take medicines, miss appointments, or cannot easily communicate with healthcare providers.

There is currently no simple platform that connects patients, doctors, volunteers, and hospitals while also supporting offline access.

---

# Vision

Our vision is to provide continuous healthcare support to people living in rural, tribal, and underserved communities through an offline-first healthcare platform.

We aim to improve medicine adherence, appointment management, and community healthcare by connecting patients, healthcare workers, hospitals, and volunteers into one simple digital ecosystem.

---

# Mission

Our mission is to build a healthcare platform that allows doctors, hospital assistants, volunteers, and patients to work together.

The platform will provide offline medicine reminders, appointment scheduling, volunteer follow-ups, and easy access to healthcare services, ensuring that no patient is left without care because of distance or lack of internet connectivity.
